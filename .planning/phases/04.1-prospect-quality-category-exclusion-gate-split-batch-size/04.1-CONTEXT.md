<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Category exclusion (revises nothing — new capability)
- **D-4.1-01: Food-service businesses are not target prospects.** Exclude or heavily down-weight the Overture "eat and drink" family (restaurants, cafes, bars) before prospects reach the shortlist. Rationale: low budget, reservation-driven — not buyers of Adashi website rebuilds. The first live shortlist was dominated by them.
- **D-4.1-02: The exclusion list is configurable, never hardcoded.** Geography and vertical are project parameters (PROJECT.md constraint). A category list constant/config, not inline strings.
- Categories already exist in the data: `prospects.category` (migration 010) and `prospect_sources.raw_category` (migration 011). No schema work expected.

### Gate split (revises D-01)
- **D-4.1-03: Unreachable prospects (`!reachable`) must NOT be releasable to the scan queue.** A scan of an unreachable site is guaranteed to fail and produces no report — and the report is the whole pitch. They may stay visible in the Shortlist table; they just cannot be released/armed. (Proven live: mollerino.nl was released while Unreachable and came back FAILED.)
- **D-4.1-04: No-HTTPS prospects (`!https`) keep the current behavior — always eligible, top priority.** A live business site without HTTPS is a prime prospect; this is the worst-first thesis working as intended.
- **D-4.1-05: Rename the "GATED" pill in the Shortlist UI to "CRITICAL"** (or equally priority-communicating). "GATED" reads as excluded when it means top priority — it misled Joshua on real data.

### Throughput (accepts the Vercel Hobby constraint)
- **D-4.1-06: BULK_BATCH_SIZE goes 2 → 10; the daily cron cadence stays.** Vercel Hobby only allows daily crons (commit 96296d9, drain at 07:00). One daily tick scanning 10 prospects = 70/week, above the 10–50/week target. 10 is the server-side clamp in `claim_next_scan_batch`, so higher values are pointless. Vercel Pro upgrade was explicitly rejected (near-zero-cost constraint). Requires production redeploy (`npx vercel --prod` — Joshua runs it; the harness cannot).

### Claude's Discretion
- Where the category exclusion sits (import-time, triage-eligibility, or both) and its exact mechanism, provided it is configurable and existing non-food prospects are unaffected.
- The concrete category string list (must cover the restaurant/cafe/bar family as observed in the real NL data — verify actual values with a query against local data before locking the list).
- How existing already-triaged food-service and unreachable rows are handled (filter at read time vs backfill/re-triage), provided the live shortlist stops showing food-service rows as releasable candidates.
- Test approach, following the project's established vitest + local-Supabase integration-test conventions (integration tests hard-pinned to 127.0.0.1).

</decisions>
