<objective>
Collapse the two divergent verdict-threshold functions into one exported from `lib/scoring.ts`,
imported by the scanner service. This is D-6-R4, locked as the FIRST plan of Phase 6: no draft
may quote a verdict externally until the prospect list, the hosted report and the drafted email
all derive it from the same function.

Purpose: DRA-06. Today `lib/scoring.ts` uses 95/85/70/50 and `scanner-service/src/index.ts` uses
90/70/50. Confirmed during planning: `lib/scoring.ts` is currently imported by nothing in the
Next.js app — the scanner service is the only live verdict producer. Migrating the scanner
service's bands INTO `lib/scoring.ts` therefore changes zero production behaviour, which is the
safe direction of travel.

Output: `computeVerdict()` + `getWeakestCategory()` exported from `lib/scoring.ts`, a first-ever
test file for that module, and a cross-boundary path alias so the scanner service consumes it.
</objective>
