<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| prospect website DOM → extractor harvest | Untrusted third-party HTML; the harvest bounds count (50) and length (50k) before anything is stored |
| harvested material → pure parser | Extracted strings are untrusted input to regex/decoding; parser bounds email length (254) and uses only linear regexes |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-03 | Denial of Service | extractor harvest of a hostile page | medium | mitigate | Cap mailtoHrefs/cfemailTokens to 50 each and contactText to 50_000 chars, bounding row growth from a pathological page |
| T-05-04 | Denial of Service | regex email/obfuscation extraction (ReDoS) | medium | mitigate | All patterns are simple, non-nested, linear-time (verified by inspection, RESEARCH Security Domain); no nested quantifiers over overlapping classes |
| T-05-05 | Tampering | oversized "email-looking" string bloating a prospects row (Phase 6 target) | medium | mitigate | aggregateContacts discards any candidate over MAX_EMAIL_LEN (254) before it can be selected |
| T-05-06 | Spoofing | crafted lookalike/third-party email captured as the prospect's own | low | mitigate | Same-domain candidates score +100 over cross-domain (Pitfall 2); ownership/deliverability validation is out of scope (send-time, Phase 8) |
</threat_model>
