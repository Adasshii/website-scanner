### AP-1 detail

**What it is.** A SUMMARY.md is the completeness signal the plan index reads. Writing one
before a plan has actually finished makes an incomplete plan indistinguishable from a
complete one, and every downstream check inherits that wrong answer.

**How it manifested.** Plan 07-07 reached a blocking human checkpoint after Task 2. The
executor correctly wrote a SUMMARY.md recording the pause and correctly returned the
checkpoint unresolved — but the two facts live in different places. `stopped_at` in
STATE.md records the truth; the plan index only sees `has_summary: true`.

**What prevents recurrence.** This file. `check_blocking_antipatterns` runs before any
other step in execute-phase and hard-stops on `severity: blocking` rows, so the gate is
re-surfaced no matter what the plan index says.
