<objective>
Deploy the retention cron and gather the four pieces of evidence that make its schedule a fact about
this project rather than a fact about Vercel's documentation.

Purpose: verification scored criterion 4 `partial` for two reasons, and this is the first one.
`vercel.json` has carried the `0 3 1 * *` entry since plan 07-07, but no deploy ever ran. Plan
07-07's own Task 3 names five steps and its SUMMARY says plainly that none of the five were
performed: the mode decision was answered directly and the evidence was never gathered. So nothing
expires on a schedule today, and nothing has ever reported what it would expire against real data.
The retention clock built in 07-06 has been proven against seeded local fixtures and against nothing
else.

This is inherently human work. It needs a production deploy and an authenticated call against live
infrastructure — authorization no executor can assume, and 07-07's continuation was explicitly told
not to assume it. So the four evidence steps are enumerated below as individually checkable criteria
rather than as one deploy-and-verify instruction, because that is exactly how they went unrun last
time.

**What this plan deliberately does not do.** It does not set `RETENTION_MODE`. The standing decision
is stay-dry-run, and the deploy ships the schedule in its non-writing state, which is the safe state.
No task here flips the mode, and the plan is written so that flipping it is not reachable by
following any instruction in it. The honest consequence: CMP-13's own verb is "expires", and a job
that reports is not a job that expires. That residual is recorded as an open item in Task 3 rather
than closed by a checkbox, which is the same call plan 07-07's SUMMARY made and the same one
07-VERIFICATION.md asks for.

Output: `.env.example` closed by hand, four recorded pieces of evidence in
`07-DEPLOY-EVIDENCE.md`, both WINDOWS.md entries resolved, and REQUIREMENTS.md saying what is true.
