<objective>
Decide which of the six trades a business actually does, by counting profile-supplied Dutch keyword
matches across weighted zones of its own site.

Purpose: the trade selects the job value, and the job value is a number an installer will check
against their own invoices. That is why no model is allowed anywhere in this path: a model naming the
trade would be a model indirectly choosing a number, which is the same failure the existing
draft-metric rule was written to prevent. The alternative, mapping the Overture category, was
measured and rejected: Overture has no heat pump, solar, kitchen or bathroom category at all.

Output: `scanner-service/src/trade-detection.ts`, a pure zone-weighted scorer, with a regression
corpus of trimmed zone objects from real sampled sites.
</objective>

## Surfaced planner assumptions (spec-less edge-probe fallback)

This plan carries the NIC-01 adjacency answer for trade scoring (an exact tie resolves by
`tieBreakRank`), which is authored as a truth in plan 09-02 where the rank lives and again here
where it is consumed. Four research assumptions are surfaced because none is closed by a test:

- **Assumption A3 (09-RESEARCH.md).** The zone weights 5/5/3/3/2/1, the minimum score of 8 and the
  dominance ratio of 1.5 were measured on a homepage-only prototype over 53 sites. A real scan reads
  up to 7 pages, so real scores will be higher and the minimum may prove too low. The mitigation is
  structural rather than predictive: all four values are tunable through the profile, and the
  15-site corpus is the regression guard that makes retuning safe.
- **Assumption A4 (09-RESEARCH.md).** Every keyword term except the ones the research verified is a
  seed. A wrong seed produces either a missed trade (the money line goes quiet, which D-9-08 and
  D-9-04 already permit) or a wrong trade (a wrong money line, which is the costlier direction).
  The Task 3 human-check puts the seeds in front of Joshua against the corpus results.
- **Assumption A7 (09-RESEARCH.md).** Heat pump is seeded to outrank central heating on an exact
  tie. If that is wrong, hybrid installers get the wrong money line. Phase 12 also receives the
  narrow confidence for exactly these sites, so it has a second signal.
- **Assumption A9 (09-RESEARCH.md).** The two-letter central-heating abbreviation was excluded as a
  term on the reasoning that it also abbreviates a curriculum vitae, without a false positive being
  observed in the sample. If the reasoning is wrong, the only cost is a missed keyword.
</surfaced_planner_assumptions>
