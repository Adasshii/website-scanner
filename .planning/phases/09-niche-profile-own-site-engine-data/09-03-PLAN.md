<objective>
Capture the Overture point and operating status at import, and add the columns the scoreable rule
needs: coordinates, the not-scoreable marker, and the reason code.

Purpose: ENG-06's third condition needs a point, Phase 10's competitor radius needs the same point,
and D-9-22's fourth condition needs a field the importer currently throws away. The work happens
once, here. The marker column is what lets a business that fails the rule keep its row and stay
readable instead of vanishing and coming straight back on the next re-import.

Output: migration 022, the coordinate and operating-status projection in the Overture query, the
fill-only-when-null upsert helper, the two new entries in the retention anonymise map, and a
blocking human checkpoint to apply the migration in production.
</objective>
