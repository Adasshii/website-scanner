<objective>
Make the scoreable rule stick: persist the reason a business was dropped, refuse to draft for a
marked prospect on both routes, re-check prospects queued before this phase, and let the importer
sample its category and region from the profile.

Purpose: ENG-06 says a failing business can never be scored or emailed. The predicate decides that
at release time; this plan covers the three paths that run outside release time, plus the folded
todo, which lands here because the profile already holds the positive category list and a random
mode that reads it is what makes adding a niche a one-file change.

Output: `lib/scoreability-refresh.ts`, a gate beside each existing rejected check, a dispatch-time
re-check, and `--category=random` / `--region=random`.
</objective>

## Surfaced planner assumptions (spec-less edge-probe fallback)

No edge-probe item is owned by this plan; ENG-06's five items are authored in plan 09-04 where the
predicate lives. Two planner assumptions are surfaced here because neither is closed by a test:

- **Open Question 6, resolved by the planner rather than by Joshua.** Already-queued prospects are
  re-checked in `dispatchOne` rather than by a one-off sweep script. The reasoning: a sweep would
  become a second place that decides scoreability, which D-9-R1 rules out, and a dispatch-time call
  is one extra read on a column the row already carries. The residual risk: a prospect that is
  mid-scan when this ships completes its scan, because the check is at dispatch and not at
  completion. That is accepted deliberately, since a completed scan without a draft costs nothing.
