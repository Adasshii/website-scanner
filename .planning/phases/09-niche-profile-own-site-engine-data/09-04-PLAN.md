<objective>
Fold the scoreable rule into `isReleasable` so one predicate in one place decides whether a business
can be scored at all, and widen the two read paths that feed it.

Purpose: ENG-06 gates everything downstream. Phase 4.1 already learned what a divergent copy of a
releasability rule costs, which is why D-9-R1 makes this an extension of the existing predicate
rather than a new one. The four conditions close the "no profile, chain, service-area, wrong match,
dead business" edge cases in one function instead of five scattered checks.

Output: `MIN_MATCH_CONFIDENCE`, `scoreableVerdict`, an extended `isReleasable`, the widened
`selectWorstN` and `getShortlist` selects, and a Shortlist that shows the reason a business was
dropped.
</objective>
