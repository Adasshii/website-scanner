<objective>
Answer the two questions a prospect's own site can answer about its commercial readiness: does it
show social proof, and can a customer actually request a quote.

Purpose: both are pitch material, and the measurement is the pitch. Machine-readable rating markup
appeared on zero of 53 sampled NL installer homepages, which means this check reads false for nearly
every prospect and that is exactly the finding the report sells. On the quote side, 30 of 53 pages
carried Dutch quote vocabulary that the scanner's existing English-only call-to-action detector
cannot see at all.

Output: the social-proof extractor and the quote-readiness evaluator in the pure Node-side module,
their types, and unit coverage including the false-positive cases the research measured.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Prospect's page text to the term matcher | Untrusted page content is matched against profile-supplied terms. Both sides of that comparison need care. |
| Profile term to the matcher | A profile string reaching a regular-expression constructor is a denial-of-service surface inside the scanner. |
| On-site rating markup to our database | A count read from a site's own markup is the site's data. A count obtained from Google is not. The distinction is a legal one. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-9-14 | Denial of service | `scanner-service/src/own-site-facts.ts` term matching | high | mitigate | Every profile term is escaped and matched as a literal, and no regular expression is constructed from a profile string or from page text. A catastrophically backtracking pattern running against untrusted page text inside the scanner would hold one of only two browser slots. The acceptance criteria assert the absence of a dynamic pattern constructor. |
| T-9-15 | Tampering with a legal constraint | the stored review count | high | mitigate | The count is stored with its source recorded as the site's own markup and is never labelled or presented as a Google figure. The ENG-07 guard is re-run in this plan and its result recorded, and the underlying legal reading is surfaced to Joshua by the Task 3 human-check rather than assumed. |
| T-9-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | package-legitimacy gate + blocking human checkpoint for [ASSUMED]/[SUS] |
