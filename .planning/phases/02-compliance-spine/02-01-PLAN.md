<objective>
Create the `suppressions` table (the single source of truth for who must not be contacted) and the `lib/suppression.ts` service that reads and writes it. This is the load-bearing foundation of the compliance spine: every other plan in this phase (unsubscribe route, webhook auto-suppression, backfill, override CLI) calls these functions.

Purpose: Make "stop" mechanically storable, matchable on both email and domain, permanent, idempotent, and impossible to silently re-add — per CMP-01/03/04/05/06 and locked decisions D-04, D-05, D-07, D-09.
Output: Migration 014, `lib/suppression.ts` (`isSuppressed`, `writeSuppression`, `liftSuppression`), a DI-stubbed unit suite, and a real-Supabase integration suite.
