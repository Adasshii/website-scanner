## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Next.js drain route → scanner-service `full-async` | Authenticated service-to-service call carrying caller-supplied `source` and `userAgent` labels |
| scanner-service → Gemini vision API | A third-party screenshot, possibly containing people, leaves the service |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-04-04 | Denial of Service | `full-async` shared Playwright capacity | high | mitigate | `isAtCapacity(activeFullScans.size, source)` refuses bulk work above `MAX_TOTAL_FULL_SCANS - RESERVED_FOR_PUBLIC` before registering the scan, so bulk load cannot exhaust the slots the live public scanner needs (SCAN-02, D-08) |
| T-04-05 | Information Disclosure | 503 refusal response body | low | mitigate | The refusal body carries only a generic message and `retryAfterSeconds`; internal counts and ceilings stay in Railway logs |
| T-04-06 | Elevation of Privilege | `source` / `userAgent` request-body fields | medium | mitigate | Both are routing/labelling inputs only. The existing `Bearer ${SCANNER_API_KEY}` check remains the sole authentication; `source: "bulk"` only ever narrows the ceiling, never widens it, and `userAgent` only reaches `browser.newContext()` |
| T-04-07 | Information Disclosure | incidental personal data in screenshots sent to Gemini | high | mitigate | `buildDesignAnalysisPrompt()` instructs the model not to describe, name, or identify any person in the screenshot; nothing person-identifying is persisted; asserted by `lib/scanner-design-prompt.test.ts` and recorded in LIA-v1.md (CMP-17, D-13) |
</threat_model>
